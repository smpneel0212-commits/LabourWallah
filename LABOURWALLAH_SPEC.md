# Labor Management App ("LabourWallah"): Full Specification & Anti-Gravity Instructions

## 1. Executive Summary & App Overview
The **LabourWallah** app is designed for contractors, site supervisors, and business owners to manage daily workforce operations. It tracks real-time labor attendance, current work assignments, new work assignments, historical work logs (yesterday and day before yesterday), and leave records.

---

## 2. Core Functional Requirements
* **Secure Authentication:** Username and password protection system modeled after standard internal dashboards (such as Paisa Bhai and IPMC tracker style).
* **Attendance Tracking:** Mark laborers as Present, Absent, Half-Day, or On Leave.
* **Work Logging & Assignment:** 
  * Live status of what work the laborer is currently doing.
  * What work was completed yesterday.
  * What work was completed the day before yesterday.
  * Interface to assign new tasks and update progress.
* **Dashboard & Search:** Filter by name, skill category, or status with summary metrics (Total laborers, Present today, On leave, Active tasks).
